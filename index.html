Somewhere
Else

Not a game. A place. Light the fire, open a window to hear the rain, walk down to the dock, or simply sit. Stay as long as you like.

· headphones recommended
⌄

Nothing to win. Everything to notice.

The cabin

Hand-built log walls, a stone fireplace that crackles, a radio playing music composed on the spot, drawers to open, a bed to rest in while the hours drift past.

The forest

Trails wind through swaying pines and birch to a tree house with a telescope and a clearing with a campfire. Deer graze at the edges. Birds talk in the morning; crickets and owls at night.

The lake

Rain stipples the water. Ducks drift past the dock. Skip a stone if you can find a smooth one. Watch the weather roll in and out.

How to be there

Keyboard & mouse

W A S D walk · Shift run · C crouch · Space jump
E use or pick up · Click throw · hold R to inspect · Q drop
F torch · Esc pause, settings & places

Touch

Drag the left side to walk, the right side to look. Tap anything to use it or pick it up. Small buttons cover jumping, crouching, running and the torch. Use the menu to travel between places.

Made in the open

Every texture, tree, sound and piece of music is generated in your browser from code — there are no downloaded assets, so nothing is borrowed and nothing is paid for. Built with three.js and cannon-es (both MIT) and the Web Audio API. The full source, documentation and a licence register are on GitHub.
